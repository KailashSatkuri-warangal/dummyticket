What you get

The downloaded PDF is a professional flight reservation document containing:

  • Passenger name matching your passport exactly (hyphens, accents, middle names supported)
  • Booking reference — 6-character alphanumeric code in airline format (a real PNR in the airline's system comes with Premium, $7.90)

  • Flight number and airline name from real airline schedules
  • Departure and arrival dates and times
  • Scannable QR code in airline-standard format
  • Document timestamp showing when generated

The dummy ticket serves as flight proof for:

  • Australia Visitor Visa (subclass 600)
  • Indian e-Visa
  • UAE / Dubai visa applications
  • Proof of onward travel checks at most international airports

Frequently asked questions

Is this video accurate as of 2026?

Yes. The MyJet24 booking form interface and PDF output match what you see in the video. The tool was last updated in 2026 to reflect current PNR formats and airline schedule data.

How long is the dummy ticket valid?

The PDF document does not expire. The dates shown on it reflect the travel dates you entered. For visa applications, generate fresh ones with your actual planned travel dates.

Are these real PNR codes?

Not on the free ticket: its 6-character booking reference is generated in the format used by airline distribution systems, but it is not held in an airline system. A real PNR in the airline's system comes with Premium ($7.90).

Is a dummy ticket legal?

Yes, when it is used for what it is. Embassies and consulates accept a flight reservation as supporting documentation — the Schengen Visa Code, for example, asks for a "reservation for a return or onward flight", not a paid ticket. What is not legal is inventing the numbers yourself, or editing a document so that it looks like a paid ticket. The MyJet24 PDF is a flight reservation and is clearly identifiable as one.

Do I need to pay anything?

No. The dummy ticket generator is completely free with no registration, no credit card, and no hidden fees. Optional premium documents (visa support letter, embassy cover letter) are paid separately.
