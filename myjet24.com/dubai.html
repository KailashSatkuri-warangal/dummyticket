What is a Dubai Dummy Ticket?

Why Do You Need a Dummy Ticket for Dubai Visa?

The United Arab Emirates and Dubai immigration authorities require all visa applicants to provide proof of onward travel. This means you must show that you have plans to leave Dubai/UAE after your visit. A dummy ticket serves as this proof without requiring you to purchase expensive, non-refundable flight tickets before your visa is approved.

Many travelers make the mistake of buying real flight tickets before receiving their Dubai visa approval, only to face rejection and lose hundreds of dollars on non-refundable tickets. Using a free Dubai dummy ticket from our generator eliminates this risk. You can submit your visa application with confidence, and only purchase real tickets after visa approval.

About DXB - Dubai International Airport

Dubai International Airport (DXB) is the primary international airport serving Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It's located in the Al Garhoud district, about 4.6 kilometers east of Dubai city center. DXB is the world's busiest airport by international passenger traffic and the third-busiest airport in the world by total passenger traffic.

The airport serves as the main hub for Emirates Airlines and flydubai. It has three terminals handling over 260 destinations worldwide. When generating your Dubai dummy ticket, always use DXB as the airport code for Dubai International Airport. This is the code recognized by all visa authorities and immigration systems globally.
