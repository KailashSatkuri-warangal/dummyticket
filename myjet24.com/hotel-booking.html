Terminology

Is a dummy hotel booking the same as a made-up hotel booking?

No, and the difference matters. People search for “dummy hotel booking” when they mean a reservation made for a visa application rather than for a stay — a document made for the application, not for the stay. A made-up hotel booking in the literal sense is an edited confirmation that pretends to come from a hotel or booking platform: invented hotels, altered documents built to deceive. Those are what consulates and fraud detection services look for, and using one is a criminal matter.

A dummy hotel booking is neither. It is a reservation document generated for your visa application — it is not held by the hotel, and it is not prepaid. If your consulate checks bookings with the hotel, use a free-cancellation booking made with the hotel or a booking platform instead.

Visa Applications

Why Do Embassies Require Proof of Accommodation?

Embassy officers need to verify that you have a place to stay during your visit. Proof of accommodation is one of the core requirements for almost every visa type worldwide. Without it, your application will be incomplete and likely rejected. The accommodation document must show hotel name, address, dates, and the applicant name matching the passport.

Crucially, embassies do not want you to prepay hotels before visa approval. They understand that non-refundable bookings create financial risk for applicants. A free-cancellation reservation or a dummy hotel booking is exactly what they expect to see. Here is what major visa authorities require:

Schengen Visa (29 European countries)
All Schengen consulates require proof of accommodation for every night of your stay. A hotel reservation, Airbnb confirmation, or invitation letter from a host is accepted. The booking does not need to be prepaid — a free cancellation reservation is the standard approach. Schengen consulates explicitly state: do not buy non-refundable bookings before visa approval.
UK Standard Visitor Visa
UKVI requires evidence of where you will stay during your visit. A hotel booking confirmation showing your name, dates, and the hotel address satisfies this. The UK Home Office guidance specifically says applicants should provide details of accommodation — not proof of payment.
US B1/B2 Tourist Visa
While not strictly mandatory at the interview, showing your hotel arrangements demonstrates genuine travel intent and significantly strengthens your application. US consular officers want to see that you have a concrete travel plan, not just a vague idea of visiting.
Canada Visitor Visa
IRCC requires a detailed travel plan including accommodation for each location. A hotel booking for every city you plan to visit is recommended. Canadian visa officers look for a coherent itinerary that matches your stated purpose of visit.
Australia ETA / e-Visa
Proof of accommodation supports your genuine visitor intent. Australian immigration expects to see evidence of planned activities and accommodation. A hotel reservation for your planned dates strengthens the application.
UAE / Dubai Tourist Visa
Hotels in the UAE often require advance booking. A hotel reservation is part of the standard application checklist for UAE tourist visas. Many UAE visa processing centers will not accept an application without an accommodation confirmation.

Why Embassies Do Not Want Prepaid Hotel Reservations

One of the most important things visa applicants must understand is that embassies explicitly discourage non-refundable hotel bookings before visa approval. The official Schengen visa application guidelines state that applicants should provide hotel reservations — not confirmed, prepaid bookings. This distinction is critical and often misunderstood by first-time applicants.

The reasoning is straightforward: if a visa is denied, the applicant loses any money spent on non-refundable hotel reservations. Embassies do not want to be responsible for creating financial hardship. They process thousands of applications with free-cancellation bookings and dummy hotel reservations every day. A hotel booking from Booking.com with free cancellation, an Airbnb reservation, or a generated accommodation confirmation document like those from MyJet24 are all common forms of proof.

The requirement itself is written into law. Annex II of the EU Visa Code — Regulation (EC) No 810/2009, the regulation that governs every Schengen visa application — lists the supporting documents a consulate may request, and for accommodation it names a reservation: “proof of accommodation”, given as a hotel reservation, lease or voucher. It does not ask for a paid stay, and nothing in the Visa Code requires one. Read Annex II of the Visa Code.

How Embassy Officers Evaluate Hotel Bookings

Understanding how visa officers review accommodation documents helps you prepare better. Officers typically spend 30-60 seconds reviewing each supporting document. They look for: (1) the guest name matching the passport exactly, (2) dates that align with the travel itinerary and flight bookings, (3) a hotel in the correct destination city, (4) a realistic accommodation choice that matches the applicant's stated financial situation, and (5) a professional format with a confirmation number.

Red flags that can trigger additional scrutiny include: dates that do not match other documents, a hotel booking for a city different from the stated destination, extremely cheap or extremely expensive hotels inconsistent with the applicant's bank statements, multiple bookings for the same nights at different hotels, and poor-quality documents with missing information. Avoid these inconsistencies by carefully cross-referencing all your visa application documents before submission.

Dummy Hotel Booking for Schengen Visa: The Complete Checklist

Schengen visa applications are the most demanding when it comes to accommodation documentation. Use this checklist to ensure your hotel bookings are complete:

1. Coverage: Hotel bookings must cover every single night from your entry date to your exit date. No gaps allowed between properties.

2. Multi-country trips: For trips across multiple Schengen countries, provide separate hotel bookings for each country with dates that connect seamlessly.

3. Main destination rule: Apply at the embassy of the country where you will spend the most nights. Your hotel bookings clearly demonstrate which country is your primary destination.

4. Matching dates: Hotel check-in dates must match your flight arrival dates. If you arrive in Paris on March 3rd, your hotel check-in should be March 3rd — not March 4th.

5. Guest details: Every traveler in the group must be named on the hotel booking. Children traveling with parents should also be listed.

6. Format: A printed PDF is preferred. The document must be clear, legible, and contain the hotel name, address, dates, guest names, and a confirmation reference.

Alternatives to Hotel Bookings: What Else Embassies Accept

While hotel bookings are the most common form of accommodation proof, embassies also accept other types of documentation. Airbnb or Booking.com reservations with free cancellation are widely accepted — just ensure the confirmation shows all required details. An invitation letter from a host in the destination country (especially common for family visit visas) can replace hotel bookings for nights spent at the host's address. For business visas, a letter from the inviting company confirming they have arranged accommodation is also accepted.

Hostel reservations, serviced apartment bookings, and even camping ground reservations are valid as long as they include the property name, address, dates, and guest names. The key is that the embassy needs to know where you will be staying each night — the type of accommodation matters less than the completeness of the documentation.

How many hotel bookings do I need for a multi-city trip?
You need proof of accommodation for every night of your trip. Do I need a hotel booking for a transit visa?
It depends on the transit country and duration. For short transits (under 24 hours) at the airport, accommodation proof is usually not required. For longer transits or if you plan to leave the airport, you may need a hotel booking. Always check the specific requirements of the transit country embassy.
Can I use a hotel booking from Booking.com for my visa?
Yes. Booking.com reservations with free cancellation are widely accepted by embassies. Many visa applicants book a refundable hotel on Booking.com, use the confirmation for the visa application, and cancel after approval. What happens if the embassy rejects my hotel booking?
If an embassy finds your accommodation proof insufficient, they will typically request additional documentation rather than reject the visa outright. Common issues include: dates not matching your travel itinerary, guest name not matching passport, or missing hotel address. Ensure all details on your hotel booking match your other visa documents exactly.
Is a hotel booking required for a US visa interview?
A hotel booking is not strictly required for a US B1/B2 visa interview, but it is highly recommended. US consular officers want to see that you have concrete travel plans. Presenting a hotel booking along with your flight itinerary demonstrates genuine travel intent and strengthens your case significantly. Many successful US visa applicants include hotel bookings in their documentation.
What is proof of accommodation for a visa?
Proof of accommodation is the document a consulate accepts as evidence of where you will stay for every night of your trip. It can be a hotel booking confirmation, a rental agreement, an invitation letter from a host, or an Airbnb reservation. What matters is that it names the address, the dates and the traveller, and that the dates leave no gap in the itinerary.
What proof of accommodation does a digital nomad visa require?
Most digital nomad visas ask for accommodation covering at least the first weeks of the stay, not the whole permit. Portugal’s D8 and Spain’s digital nomad visa accept a rental contract or a hotel booking; Croatia and Estonia accept a booking confirmation for the initial period. Because long-stay plans usually change, a free booking confirmation for the opening weeks is the common way applicants meet the requirement without committing money.
How do I show proof of accommodation without paying for a hotel?
Three routes are accepted. A free-cancellation reservation on a booking platform, which you cancel after approval. An invitation letter from a host, which in France and Germany takes an official form (attestation d’accueil, Verpflichtungserklaerung). Or a hotel booking confirmation generated for the application, which is what MyJet24 produces — no card, no prepayment, no cancellation to remember.
Does an Airbnb reservation count as proof of accommodation?
Usually yes — consulates accept Airbnb for visa applications, provided the confirmation carries the full address, the exact dates, the guest name and the host details. Consulates reject Airbnb proof when the listing shows only a district instead of a street address, or when the guest name does not match the passport. If the host cannot supply a formal confirmation, a hotel booking is the safer document.
What does a proof of accommodation document look like?
It is a one-page confirmation carrying six things: the hotel or property name, the full postal address, check-in and check-out dates, the guest name exactly as in the passport, a booking reference number, and the room type or rate. A document missing any of the six is what officers query most often.
Is proof of accommodation required for a Schengen visa?
Yes. The Schengen Visa Code lists accommodation evidence among the supporting documents, and it must cover every night of the intended stay. It does not have to be prepaid — the consulates explicitly do not expect applicants to commit money before a decision.
