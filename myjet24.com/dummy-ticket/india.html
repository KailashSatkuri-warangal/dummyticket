Visa-Embassy Focus

A dummy ticket for India is the document you submit to the embassy or VFS centre with your visa application. What India Embassy Officers Look For on Your Dummy Ticket

The India embassy reviewer wants: (1) full passenger name matching your passport application, (2) flight dates that fall WITHIN the visa-validity window you requested, (3) a round-trip itinerary if your visa category is short-stay, (4) a real-format PNR / booking reference, and (5) IATA-code airports rather than city names alone. Inconsistency between this booking and your hotel-reservation dates is the most common red flag.

The Online Application

India e-Visa Application — Complete Walkthrough at indianvisaonline.gov.in

The India e-Visa is unique among major visa systems — it is 100% online, no embassy interview required. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa. Decision arrives by email within 3-5 working days. The full process takes 30-45 minutes to complete the 16-section form. Below is the step-by-step walkthrough every applicant should follow.

India e-Visa Step-by-Step Application

  1. Verify e-Visa eligibility — your nationality must be on the e-Visa list (170+ countries including US, UK, EU, Australia, Canada, Japan, South Korea, etc.). Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Afghan, Chinese, certain Iranian and Iraqi passport holders need a regular visa.
  2. Visit indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa — the ONLY official portal. Beware third-party sites charging USD 50-150 for the same free application service.
  3. Choose visa type — Tourist (30-day/1-year/5-year), Business, Medical, Conference, or Medical Attendant. Tourist is most common.
  4. Upload a 350×350 pixel photo — white background, JPEG format, 10 KB to 1 MB file size, taken within last 3 months. Reject conditions: glasses, smile, hair covering face, off-white background.
  5. Scan and upload passport bio page — PDF format, max 300 KB, all 4 corners visible, no glare, all text readable.
  6. Complete the 16-section form — personal info, passport details, current address, employment, family details, expected arrival/departure dates, places to be visited in India, prior India visit history.
  7. Provide accommodation details — full hotel address with name, OR host invitation letter for friends/family. "Hotel in Delhi" is rejected — be specific.
  8. Pay the fee — USD 25 (30-day), USD 40 (1-year), USD 80 (5-year) by credit/debit card. Payment gateway fee adds USD 2.50-3.50.
  9. Save the Application ID — temporary application number to retrieve incomplete submissions within 7 days.
  10. Wait for decision by email — 3-5 working days typical. Approved e-Visa arrives as PDF.
  11. Print the ETA approval — carry alongside your passport. Air carrier check-in agents verify ETA against your passenger record before allowing boarding.
Beware Third-Party Scam Sites
Multiple websites with names like "indianevisa.org" or "india-evisa-application.com" charge USD 50-150 for the same e-Visa application that costs USD 25 at the official portal. They re-submit your data to the official Indian system and pocket the difference. Only indianvisaonline.gov.in is the official Government of India portal.

Photo Specifications — 8 Common Rejection Reasons

  1. Wrong file size — must be JPEG, 10 KB minimum, 1 MB maximum. Phone photos default to 5+ MB, must be compressed.
  2. Wrong dimensions — exactly 350×350 pixels (square format). 600×800 phone selfies are auto-rejected.
  3. Background not pure white — off-white, cream, blue, or patterned backgrounds fail. Use plain white wall or white sheet.
  4. Glasses (since 2016) — all eyewear banned including reading glasses, sunglasses, photochromic lenses.
  5. Hair covering face / forehead — both ears AND forehead must be fully visible. Pin back fringe.
  6. Smile or open mouth — neutral expression required, lips closed, no teeth visible.
  7. Head tilted — must look directly at camera, head level. No three-quarter angle.
  8. Shadow on face — even lighting, no harsh shadows under chin or behind head.

Regular Paper Visa Application Process

  1. Complete online application form at indianvisaonline.gov.in/visa
  2. Print application + appointment confirmation
  3. Book biometric appointment at designated embassy/IVAC
  4. Submit application + supporting documents in person (passport, photos, application fee, financial proof, ITR, sponsor letter for Pakistani applicants)
  5. Provide biometric data (fingerprints + photo) at the embassy
  6. Pay visa fee at embassy cashier or via designated bank deposit
  7. Wait for processing (varies by nationality — 2–16 weeks)
  8. Collect passport with stamped visa from embassy/IVAC

OCI & PIO Cards

OCI & PIO Cards — Lifetime Visa Alternative for People of Indian Origin

Foreign nationals of Indian origin — and their foreign-passport spouses — qualify for the Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) card. The OCI is functionally equivalent to a lifetime multi-entry visa, plus broader rights (property ownership, business setup, no police registration). The legacy Person of Indian Origin (PIO) card was merged into the OCI scheme in 2015, but existing PIO cards remain valid.

Who Qualifies for OCI?

  1. Foreign citizens who were eligible to become Indian citizens on or after 26 January 1950
  2. Foreign citizens who held Indian citizenship at any time on or after 26 January 1950
  3. Children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren of (1) or (2)
  4. Foreign-passport spouses of Indian citizens or OCI cardholders (marriage 2+ years, varies)
  5. Minor children where at least one parent qualifies

Common Mistakes

10 Common India e-Visa Application Mistakes That Cause Rejection

Based on Bureau of Immigration rejection patterns and traveler-reported issues across 2024-2025, these are the 10 mistakes that cause most preventable India e-Visa rejections. Avoid all 10 and your application moves to the merit-review stage where approval rates exceed 95%.

  1. Wrong photo size or background. Must be exactly 350×350 pixels, white background, JPEG format, 10 KB-1 MB. Phone selfies fail size check. Use professional photo or photoaid.com auto-cropping tool.
  2. Name mismatch between application and passport. Surname/given name order, middle name omission, spelling errors — all trigger automatic rejection. Type EXACTLY as in passport.
  3. Vague accommodation address. "Hotel in Delhi" rejected. Use full hotel name + street + city. For homestay, use host’s complete residential address.
  4. Wrong visa type for purpose. Applying for Tourist e-Visa for medical treatment, business meetings, or conference attendance = automatic rejection. Match purpose to visa class.
  5. Failure to disclose prior visa history. All prior India visas, refusals, or deportations must be disclosed. The BoI database tracks history — undisclosed events cause permanent rejection.
  6. Applying with passport < 6 months remaining. India requires 6+ months passport validity. Renew before applying.
  7. Passport scan with corners cropped. All 4 corners of the bio page must be visible. Cropped scans rejected on document review.
  8. Onward ticket missing or expired. The e-Visa requires planned departure flight details. A MyJet24 dummy ticket satisfies this — free PDF with booking reference in 30 seconds, or a real PNR with Premium ($7.90).
  9. Applying for e-Visa from ineligible nationality. Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Afghan, Chinese — they cannot use e-Visa. The system rejects the application automatically.
  10. Applying through third-party scam sites. Sites with names like "india-evisa-official.com" or "india-evisa.org" charge USD 50-150 and re-submit your data. Use only indianvisaonline.gov.in.

India — frequently asked questions

How quickly can I have the document?
Half a minute, which is the point when the ETA has not come through yet and the application form is asking for dates. You need something to put in the box before you know whether the authorisation will be granted, and a held booking gives you that without committing a fare to a decision nobody has made.
How long does the reservation need to stay valid?
Long enough to cover the e-Visa processing plus the gap until you fly. Processing usually runs a few days and there is no expedited lane worth counting on. After that, what matters is that the departure date still describes the trip you are taking — a booking left over from an earlier plan is worse than no booking at the counter.
Does everyone travelling need their own?
Yes, one per passport, because each ETA is issued against one name and the spelling has to match. Indian systems read the transliteration literally: a middle name present on the passport and absent on the booking is enough to stop a match. Generate them separately and check each against the passport page.
Do I need the hotel confirmation too?
The e-Visa form asks for an address in India, and the hotel files a C-form for you once you check in — so the address is doing work on both ends. What matters is that it fits the flights: an address in Kerala beside an arrival at Delhi and a departure two days later describes a trip that does not exist.
Will anyone actually check the booking reference?
At the departure gate, if the agent decides to. They type it into their own terminal and it either resolves or it does not — nobody telephones anybody. That is also why a screenshot of a booking page carries no weight there: there is no reference to enter, so there is nothing to verify.
What does Premium change?
Whether the reference exists in the airline's system as well as on the PDF. For India that shows up at check-in abroad rather than at the Indian border, where the officer is matching your passport against a granted authorisation. On a long-haul round trip into Delhi it rarely comes up; on a one-way into Goa in season it comes up more.
Does it matter which of the 31 airports I choose?
For the e-Visa it matters absolutely — arrive at an airport that is not on the list and the authorisation does not admit you. Beyond that the choice is practical: Delhi and Mumbai for connections, Bengaluru and Chennai for the south, Goa for the coast. Departure is unrestricted, so only the arrival airport has to be from the list.
Is showing a reservation rather than a ticket legitimate?
Yes, as long as it is described as what it is. A held booking is genuine — the airline system holds it — and calling it a reservation is accurate. Altering the PDF, inventing a reference or presenting it as paid would be a different matter, and a false statement to an Indian immigration officer stands on its own regardless of what happens to the visa.
Does the e-Visa application at indianvisaonline.gov.in ask for a flight booking?
It asks for your intended arrival and departure and for an address in India, and the form is unforgiving about consistency: what you type is what the officer sees when you land. A held booking makes those dates checkable without buying a fare before the ETA exists. The approval arrives by email as an Electronic Travel Authorization; the visa itself is stamped into the passport on arrival.
How much does the Indian e-Tourist Visa cost?
It depends on the length and, unusually, on the season. The 30-day visa is USD 25 from July to March and drops to USD 10 from April to June. The one-year is USD 40 and the five-year USD 80. That April-to-June window is the low season for tourism and the discount is deliberate — worth knowing if your dates are flexible by a few weeks.
What is the difference between the 30-day, one-year and five-year e-Visa?
The 30-day is double entry and runs 30 days from the date you arrive. The one-year and five-year are multiple entry and run from the date the authorisation is granted, not from arrival — so a one-year visa issued in March expires the following March whether or not you have used it. That distinction catches people out when they plan a second trip.
How much passport validity does India want?
Six months beyond arrival and at least two blank pages — two, not one. The second page matters because the visa is stamped in on arrival rather than printed at home, and a passport with a single free page can be turned back at the counter with a valid ETA in hand.
Can I land at any airport with an e-Visa?
No, and this is the requirement most often missed. The e-Visa is valid only at 31 designated airports — Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Goa at both Dabolim and Mopa, Kochi, Jaipur, Varanasi, Amritsar, Port Blair and the rest — plus about five seaports. Arriving anywhere else with an e-Visa means arriving without a valid entry route.
Does the departure have to be from a designated airport too?
No. The restriction applies to entry, not exit — you may leave through any authorised immigration post. That gives a booking more freedom than the arrival side: land at Delhi, leave from Goa or overland to Nepal, and the e-Visa still holds.
What has to be on the reservation itself?
The name exactly as the passport prints it, a dated departure out of India, the operating carrier and a reference that resolves. Indian officials read the passport transliteration literally, so a name that differs by a middle initial from what the ETA says is a real problem — not because anyone suspects you, but because the systems will not match it.
Free or Premium for an Indian application?
The portal reads the fields either way. Where the tier shows is the departure gate abroad, if an agent decides to query the reference in the airline's own system. On long-haul routes into Delhi and Mumbai with a return in the booking that is uncommon; on one-way arrivals into Goa in season it is less uncommon.
What happens if the ETA does not arrive before I fly?
You do not board. The airline checks for a granted ETA at check-in because carrying a passenger without one makes them liable for the return. Processing is usually a few days, but there is no counter to appeal to at the airport — which is the argument for booking the reservation rather than the ticket while the application is still open.
