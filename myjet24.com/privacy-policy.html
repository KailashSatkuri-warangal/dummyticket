Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Who We Are

MyJet24.com ("we", "us", "our") operates the website myjet24.com. We provide dummy flight ticket, hotel reservation, and travel document generation services for visa applications and travel planning purposes.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal data:

  • Contact information: Name, email address (when you use our contact form or services)
  • Travel details: Departure/arrival cities, travel dates, passenger names (entered by you to generate documents)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, browser type, device information, IP address (collected automatically)
  • Cookies: Session cookies, analytics cookies (Google Analytics), preference cookies

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To generate dummy flight tickets, hotel reservations, and other travel documents as requested by you
  • To respond to your inquiries via the contact form
  • To improve our website and services through usage analytics
  • To ensure the security and proper functioning of our website

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (session management, CSRF protection)
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics (G-TPMSBEKSZB) to understand how visitors use our site
  • Preference cookies: To remember your settings such as dark/light theme

You can control cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner or your browser settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Google Analytics: For website usage analysis (anonymized)
  • Hosting provider: Hostinger, for server infrastructure

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. The passport number is automatically removed from stored documents after 180 days; the document itself remains available.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data
  • Data portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw cookie consent at any time

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

For any questions or requests regarding your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: support@myjet24.com
  • Website: myjet24.com/about (contact form)
