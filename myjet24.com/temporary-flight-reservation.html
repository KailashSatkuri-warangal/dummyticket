What is a temporary flight reservation?
A PDF itinerary with your route, travel dates, passenger names and a booking reference, created without buying a ticket. Many consulates ask for exactly this at application stage. It is also known as a dummy ticket.
Is it a booking in the airline’s system?
Free: no — it is a reservation document for your application file, not a paid ticket and not a held seat, and its booking reference identifies your MyJet24 document. Premium: yes — it is booked with a real PNR in the airline’s system and held for 24 to 72 hours.
How long is a temporary flight reservation valid?
Free: the document shows the dates you enter and does not expire. Premium: the real PNR stays in the airline’s system for 24 to 72 hours, so create it close to your appointment.
How much does a verifiable flight reservation cost?
With MyJet24 Premium it costs $7.90 one-time: a real PNR in the airline’s system, valid 24 to 72 hours, with the PDF instantly and a copy by email. The free version with a booking reference costs nothing.
Will the embassy accept it?
Consulates ask for proof of intended travel, not proof of purchase. Requirements differ by country, so check the guide for yours before you submit.
What is the difference between Free and Premium?
Both contain the full itinerary. Free uses a sample flight format with a MyJet24 watermark. Premium uses a real airline and flight number from the current schedule, has no watermark, and a copy is emailed to you.
Can I reserve a flight without paying the airline?
Many airlines let you hold a fare for 24 to 72 hours, usually for a small fee. A temporary reservation from MyJet24 needs no airline payment at all.
Can I change dates or names later?
Create a new reservation with the corrected details. Check the spelling against your passport before you generate it.
When do I need a real ticket instead?
For check-in and boarding, for transit visas that require a confirmed onward booking, and for travel-expense claims. For airline checks at departure, see onward ticket.
