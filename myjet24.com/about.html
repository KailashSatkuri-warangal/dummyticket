Our Story

How It All Started

MyJet24 was founded in 2021 in London with a simple mission: make visa applications easier for travelers around the world. We noticed that thousands of people were paying unnecessary fees for flight reservations they only needed temporarily – just to satisfy embassy requirements.

So we built a platform that generates dummy tickets and flight reservations instantly, for free — and verified reservations with Premium ($7.90). What started as a small project quickly grew into a trusted service used by over 10,000 travelers across 195 countries.

Today, MyJet24 is one of the leading platforms for dummy tickets, onward tickets, and flight itineraries for visa applications. Whether you're applying for a Schengen visa, a US tourist visa, or any other travel document – we provide flight reservations with valid PNR codes for your visa application, delivered in minutes.

Our team is passionate about travel and technology. We believe everyone deserves access to affordable travel documentation, regardless of where they come from.

How It Works

How MyJet24 Premium Generates Verified Flight Reservations

A transparent look at the technology and process behind every dummy ticket we deliver.

When you order Premium through MyJet24, our system connects to the same global airline distribution systems (GDS) used by professional travel agencies worldwide. Within seconds, we create a real, time-bound reservation on a live flight schedule and assign a unique six-character PNR (Passenger Name Record) code. This is not a Photoshop edit or an invented itinerary template — it is a verifiable booking that exists in the airline's reservation system. The free version gives you a PDF with a booking reference and QR code, ready instantly.

The PDF you receive is generated automatically using the airline's official format, including booking reference, passenger name, route, dates, fare class, and reservation status. Embassies and consular officers can verify the reservation by entering the PNR into the airline's own "Manage Booking" tool — which is exactly what most embassies do during interview screening. Because the booking is real, it passes verification consistently for visa interviews, immigration questioning, and proof-of-onward-travel checks at the airport.

Premium reservations stay live in the airline system for 24–72 hours, then expire automatically. We never charge a cancellation fee, never auto-renew, and never request bank account information. If you need a longer hold for a specific embassy timeline, our support team can extend the reservation manually at no cost. Premium add-ons such as visa support letters, hotel reservations, and travel itineraries are clearly marked and priced — never bundled silently.

Behind the platform sits a fault-tolerant infrastructure with redundant connections to multiple GDS providers, automated PDF generation, and 24/7 monitoring. The system has processed over 10,000 reservations since 2021 with industry-leading uptime. All traffic is served over HTTPS, request data is purged within 24 hours of delivery, and we never sell or share customer information with third parties.

Our Commitment

Privacy, Accuracy, and Editorial Standards

The principles that guide every product decision and every line of content we publish.

Privacy by Default

We collect the minimum data needed to generate your flight reservation — name, email, route, and dates. We never ask for passport scans, bank details, or government IDs. Your data is purged within 24 hours of ticket delivery, never shared with advertisers, and never used to train AI models. Read our full privacy policy for details on cookies, analytics, and your data rights under GDPR and CCPA.

Editorial Accuracy

Every visa guide, country page, and embassy requirement on MyJet24 is researched against official government sources — embassy websites, consular bulletins, and the IATA Travel Centre. Our visa consultants review high-traffic pages quarterly to catch policy changes such as new e-visa launches, fee updates, or document revisions. Where official sources contradict each other, we cite both and recommend confirming with the embassy directly.

Corrections Policy

If you find an error on any MyJet24 page — an outdated visa fee, a wrong embassy address, a broken link, or an inaccurate processing time — email corrections@myjet24.com with the URL and the issue. We respond within one business day and update the page publicly with a "Last updated" date. We treat editorial accuracy as a trust signal, not an afterthought, and we welcome reader feedback as a critical input to keeping our guides reliable.
