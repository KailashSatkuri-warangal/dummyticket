About Marc

Marc Hoffmann is the founder and lead visa consultant at MyJet24, a platform he created in 2021 to solve a problem he experienced firsthand: the stress and confusion of obtaining dummy flight tickets and travel documentation for visa applications.

With expertise spanning visa requirements across 195+ countries, Marc has personally guided over 50,000 travelers through the visa application process. His deep understanding of embassy requirements, Schengen visa regulations, and proof-of-onward-travel policies comes from years of working directly with applicants and tracking policy changes across consulates worldwide.

Marc's mission is to make travel documentation accessible and free for everyone. Every article on MyJet24 is written from firsthand research and real-world experience helping travelers get their visas approved.
