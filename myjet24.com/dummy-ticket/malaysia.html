What Malaysia expects from a visitor, and who checks it first

Malaysia’s conditions for a visitor are brief: a passport valid for six months, a return or onward ticket and enough money for the visit. The airline checks the ticket first, because it has to carry back anyone the counter refuses. Overstaying by even a few days can end in detention and a ban, so the date out deserves more care than the date in.
