About James

James Mitchell is the CEO and Founder of MyJet24, the leading travel tools platform serving travelers across 190+ countries with visa checkers, dummy ticket generation, embassy finders and professional travel documentation.

Based in Dubai, James founded MyJet24 with a clear mission: to remove the barriers and frustrations travelers face when navigating visa applications and travel documentation requirements. Under his leadership, MyJet24 has grown to serve over 50,000 travelers with free and premium tools.

James regularly shares insights and in-depth guides on visa applications, travel planning, and documentation requirements. His hands-on experience with international travel regulations across the Middle East, Europe, Asia and the Americas gives him a unique perspective on the challenges travelers face worldwide.
