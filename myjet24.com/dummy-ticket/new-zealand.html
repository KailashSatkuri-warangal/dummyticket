Visa-Embassy Focus

A dummy ticket for New Zealand is the document you submit to the embassy or VFS centre with your visa application. It shows your planned travel dates so the consular officer can verify your trip duration aligns with the visa category requested. This page focuses on the embassy-submission requirements, processing-time considerations, and visa-fee context for New Zealand.

What New Zealand Embassy Officers Look For on Your Dummy Ticket

The New Zealand embassy reviewer wants: (1) full passenger name matching your passport application, (2) flight dates that fall WITHIN the visa-validity window you requested, (3) a round-trip itinerary if your visa category is short-stay, (4) a real-format PNR / booking reference, and (5) IATA-code airports rather than city names alone. Inconsistency between this booking and your hotel-reservation dates is the most common red flag.

Application Walkthrough

NZ NZeTA + Visitor Visa + Skilled Migrant Walkthrough at INZ (2026)

New Zealand operates: NZeTA (NZD 23) + IVL (International Visitor Levy) NZD 35 = ~NZD 58 total — required for ~60 visa-waiver nationalities since 2019. Visitor Visa for visa-required nationalities. Long-term: Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) Resident Visa, Working Holiday, Active Investor Plus Visa (replaced Investor 1 & 2).

Skilled Migrant Reform 2024

Skilled Migrant 6-Point System (Oct 2023 Reform)

NZ Skilled Migrant Category reformed October 2023 — replaced 160-point system with 6-point system. Need 6 points from: New Zealand registration/license (1-3 pts), occupation qualification (1-3 pts), income (1-3 pts), work experience (1-2 pts). No more skill-shortage list — focus on income + Kiwi credentials.

New Zealand — frequently asked questions

How fast is it, and does that help on a New Zealand booking?
Half a minute. It helps most in the place people forget: the check-in desk in Singapore, Dubai or Los Angeles, thirteen hours before you reach New Zealand. That agent is the one who reads the entry conditions and decides whether to board you, and they are not going to take your word for the return leg.
How long does the reservation stay useful?
Through check-in and the arrivals hall at Auckland or Christchurch. Set the date against the six months a visitor gets, and remember the ceiling behind it — no more than six months in any rolling twelve, whatever your NZeTA says about two years.
Does each traveller need their own?
One per passport, spelled as the passport spells it. The same is true of everything else New Zealand asks for: each person needs their own NZeTA, pays their own NZ$100 levy and files their own Traveller Declaration. Nothing here is issued to a family.
Is a hotel booking part of what they look at?
Frequently, because it sits alongside the funds question the entry conditions actually name. An address for the first nights plus a departure date plus a means of paying is the complete answer, and New Zealand's officers ask for all three more often than most.
Will an agent verify the booking reference?
At check-in for a long-haul sector, yes, more often than for a short one. The airline carries the liability for anyone refused entry at the far end, and thirteen hours of fuel makes that liability expensive. A reference that resolves in their system is what ends the question.
Is Premium worth it here?
Yes, and more clearly than for most destinations. The return ticket is named in the entry conditions rather than implied, the flights are long and costly to rebook, and the check happens before departure. Of everywhere in this catalogue, New Zealand is among the strongest cases for the paid tier.
Does the arrival airport change anything?
For transit it changes everything. Auckland has the country's only airside transit area, so a connection there can run up to 24 hours without an NZeTA; Christchurch, Wellington and Queenstown put you through the border and require one. For an ordinary arrival the rules are identical everywhere.
Is a held reservation an honest thing to show?
Yes, as a reservation. The condition is evidence of onward travel, not evidence of payment, and a dated booking is the ordinary form that evidence takes. Altering the document or calling it a purchased ticket would be a false statement to an immigration officer — and in a country that fines an undeclared apple, that is not a place to be creative.
Is the onward ticket an actual requirement in New Zealand?
It is stated outright: you may be asked for proof of a return or onward ticket and proof that you have enough money for your stay. Both sit in the entry conditions rather than in an officer's discretion, and both are asked for more readily here than in most places — an island at the end of a long-haul route has an obvious interest in knowing you can leave it.
What is the NZeTA and what does it cost?
An electronic travel authority, and the price depends on where you apply: NZ$17 through the app, NZ$23 through the website. Six dollars is not much, but the app is also the faster route. Allow 72 hours for processing regardless — the approval is usually quicker and occasionally is not.
How long does an NZeTA last?
Two years for travellers, five for crew, covering as many visits as you like inside that window. There is a ceiling on the visiting itself, though: no more than six months in any rolling twelve. The authority lasts two years; your welcome does not.
What is the levy everyone talks about?
The International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy — NZ$100 a person, paid at the same time as the NZeTA. It is not refunded if the application is declined, which is the detail worth knowing before you apply with anything questionable in your history.
What else do I have to file before I fly?
The New Zealand Traveller Declaration, and everybody arriving needs one. It is free. Flying, you can submit it from 24 hours before your trip starts until you reach passport control; arriving by sea, from 24 hours before the vessel leaves its last foreign port. It is separate from the NZeTA and neither replaces the other.
What passport validity do I need?
Your passport must expire at least three months after the day you plan to leave — leave, not arrive. It is a milder rule than most of Asia applies, and it catches people precisely because they are used to the six-month version.
What has to be on the reservation?
The name as the passport prints it, a dated departure from New Zealand, the operating carrier and a reference that resolves. Given that the return ticket is named in the entry conditions, this is a destination where a document that survives being typed into a system earns its cost.
Free or Premium for New Zealand?
Premium, in most cases. The requirement is written down rather than implied, the flights are long and expensive to rebook, and a check-in agent in Singapore or Dubai putting you on a fourteen-hour sector will want the departure evidence to look solid. This is not the place to save six dollars.
How long can I actually stay?
Up to six months for visitors from the countries with the longer allowance, with the twelve-month ceiling above sitting over the top of it. That is generous by regional standards, and the generosity is exactly why the border asks about your means and your ticket rather than waving people through.
